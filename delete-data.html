FeELINE - Data Deletion

FeELINE is an AI-based cat emotion recognition application. We provide users with the ability to delete certain data without deleting their entire FeELINE account.

How to Delete a Cat Profile

To delete a cat profile while keeping your FeELINE account active:

  1. Open the FeELINE application.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Navigate to your cat profiles.
  4. Select the cat profile you want to delete.
  5. Choose the Delete option.
  6. Confirm the deletion.

What Data Is Deleted?

Deleting a cat profile removes the selected cat profile record from the FeELINE database, including its stored profile information.

What Data Is Retained?

Deleting a cat profile does not delete your FeELINE account or other cat profiles. Other account-related information remains associated with your account.

Associated images, detection records, and backup copies may be subject to separate deletion or retention processes. Please refer to the FeELINE Privacy Policy for further details.

Account Deletion

If you wish to delete your entire FeELINE account and its associated data, visit our Account Deletion page .
