FeELINE - Account Deletion

FeELINE is an AI-based cat emotion recognition application. Users can request deletion of their FeELINE account and associated personal data.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the FeELINE mobile application.
  2. Sign in using your Google account.
  3. Navigate to the account settings.
  4. Select the "Delete Account" option.
  5. Follow the confirmation instructions.

Data Associated with Account Deletion

Account deletion is intended to remove the user's FeELINE account and associated data, including:

  • Account profile information
  • Saved cat information and records
  • Associated cat images stored in Cloudinary
  • Associated emotion detection records

Processing and Retention

Account deletion and image cleanup may be processed separately. Image cleanup may remain pending after account deletion.

Any information that must be retained for legal, security, or operational reasons will be handled according to the application's applicable privacy policy.

Additional Information

Deleting your FeELINE account does not delete your Google account.
