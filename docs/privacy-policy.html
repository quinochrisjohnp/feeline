FeELINE

Privacy Policy

This page reflects the Privacy Policy currently displayed in the FeELINE application. It may be updated as app features and data-handling practices are reviewed.

Google Login

FeELINE uses account authentication to access your cat profiles and detection history. Sign-in information is exchanged with the authentication service.

Image Data

Photos submitted for detection are sent to the configured service for analysis. Successful results and saved images are available through your account.

Cat Profile Data

Cat profile information and detection history are stored through the application service. Reloading the app does not reset your saved account data.

Data Usage

Profile and image data support cat profiles, image analysis, albums, and detection history. Wellness reminders and their presentation state are stored locally on this device.

Data Deletion

You can manage cats and remove saved photos through the app. Removing a photo from an Album does not delete its detection history. Logging out ends the local session.

Security

Session credentials use SecureStore on supported native devices. Keep your device and sign-in details secure.

Consent

By continuing to use FeELINE, you acknowledge the data and session behavior described in this Privacy Policy.
